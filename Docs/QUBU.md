#Qubu

> Qubu is a  AI/ML platform: models, datasets, laptops, courses and collaborative research.

## Core sections

- [Models](https://qubu.ai/models): catalogue of ML models with metrics and weights

- [Datasets](https://qubu.ai/datasets): datasets with descriptions and diagrams

- [Notebooks](https://qubu.ai/notebooks): interactive Jupyter laptops

- [Learning](https://qubu.ai/learning): courses and training materials

- [Journal](https://qubu.ai/journal): articles and research

- [Forum](https://qubu.ai/forum): discussions and Q&A

- [Events](https://qubu.ai/events): conferences and meetups

- [AI Solutions](https://qubu.ai/ai-solutions): ready-made AI solutions for business
